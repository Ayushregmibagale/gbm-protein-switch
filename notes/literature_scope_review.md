# What the cited primary evidence supports

Koeber et al. establish SSE-mediated transcriptional selectivity and report higher-order
TF recruitment dependent on enhancer architecture and cooperating factors. They also
observe complex formation with SOX2/SOX9 in engineered fibroblasts, weaker than in
GSCs. The paper therefore does not establish that a two-protein coincidence detector
alone is GSC-specific, nor validate this engineered caspase actuator. The study's
payload results belong to its tested SSE system and cannot be transferred to this
project's computational constructs. [Nature primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10329-6)

The experimental CARD-deleted caspase-9 structure and its primary study concern
dimer-associated activation and distinct conformations. Here, one large/small
fragment pair represents a single catalytic core. Inference: proximity of those
fragments does not establish the functional dimeric context. Neither that reference
nor the current MD supplies validation of an engineered coincidence detector split
at D315/A316. [1JXQ and its primary citation](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1JXQ)

The reference-fold audit is a structural comparison. It cannot serve as an activity
classifier, and the supplied literature does not make a computational non-leakiness
or treatment-readiness result available.
