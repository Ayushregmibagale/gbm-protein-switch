# Research notes and current evidence

Historical campaign notes are preserved as records of the reasoning and results
available at their stated dates. Their readiness statements, thresholds and lead
rankings are not current validated conclusions.

Current findings are in `../ROBUSTNESS_REVIEW.md`, `../COMPUTATIONAL_STATUS.json`,
and the versioned evidence directories. `literature_scope_review.md` records the
scope of primary sources consulted during the robustness review. The other projects
mentioned in historical comparison notes have not been audited in this task.
